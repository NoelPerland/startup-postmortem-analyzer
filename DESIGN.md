# Startup Analyzer design

- Role: help readers investigate a startup with a reviewable draft, timeline, factors and open questions.
- Voice: editorial and evidence-led. Separate facts, hypotheses and missing evidence. Label demo mode clearly.
- Type: existing serif for article titles, sans for controls and body reading. Avoid tiny tracking-heavy form labels.
- Color: warm paper, navy text and blue interactive accents; both themes must preserve contrast.
- Layout: one research form, then article output. No nested cards or duplicate calls to action.
- Motion: progress feedback only; never make evidence appear fabricated by animation.
